    How scoring works
    • Spot on: 100 points. Close: 60 points. Wrong: 0.
    • Answer fast for up to +20 points.
    • Each perfect answer in a row adds +10 more, up to +50.
    • Help costs points: a hint keeps 60% of the base points, more help keeps 30%.
    • Showing teammates costs points: Setter keeps 70% of every point, Everyone keeps 30%.
