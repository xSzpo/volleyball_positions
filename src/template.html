How to learn with this app
  1. Study once, then stop rereading. Pick your role and step through R1 (S1) to R6 (S2) here in Learn. For each rotation, say the four checks out loud.
  2. Test yourself. Go to Drill with the setter shown. Guess first, then look at the answer. Mistakes are part of how you learn.
  3. Play a Match in order, then mixed. When you score 80% or more, switch to mixed order and show nobody. Mixing the order makes you think, which helps you remember.
  4. Little and often. Five minutes on three or four days a week works better than one long session. Start each session with “Review weak spots” in Drill.
  5. Before training or a match, do a two-minute review of the role you will play. If you might play several roles, practise each one.
  6. Finish on court. The app gets you ready; walking through the rotations with your team makes it stick. Your coach and the KSV guide have the final word.
Before every serve

Four quick checks, in this order. Use them in the app and on court.

  1. Where is the setter? That tells you the rotation.
  2. Who is in front of or behind me, and who is beside me? That confirms your legal spot.
  3. Am I on court, or is the libero in? Middles, check this every time.
  4. What is my job after the pass? Receive, attack which lane, set, or cover.
